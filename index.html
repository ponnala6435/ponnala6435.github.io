🧠 What is Primo?

Primo is an extraordinary open-source humanoid robot designed for hobbyists, researchers, and educators alike. This incredible machine combines the power of 3D printing with advanced robotics technology, featuring 30 high-performance RobStride actuators that give it remarkable movement and dexterity. Powered by the cutting-edge NVIDIA Jetson Thor, Primo can process complex instructions and execute bipedal movements with precision.

Currently in the design phase, Primo represents the future of accessible robotics. We're building a community of innovators who believe that advanced robotics should be available to everyone. With detailed design files, comprehensive documentation, and a supportive community, you'll have everything you need to bring Primo to life.

✨ Amazing Features

🖨️ Complete 3D Printed Design

Every structural component of Primo can be 3D printed at home. We provide optimized STL files and Onshape documents for easy customization. Save thousands compared to commercial humanoids!

⚡ 30 RobStride Actuators

Experience smooth, natural movement with 30 quasi-direct-drive actuators. Each actuator provides high torque while remaining lightweight and power-efficient.

🧠 Jetson Thor Computing

Run sophisticated AI models and reinforcement learning algorithms directly on-board. Perfect for Isaac Lab and Isaac Sim integration.

🤖 Advanced Bipedal Locomotion

Walk, balance, and navigate like a human. Primo's sophisticated control systems and URDF model make it perfect for testing cutting-edge robotics research.

🌍 Fully Open Source

All design files, schematics, and code are freely available under open-source licenses. Modify, improve, and share your creations with the global community.

📚 Comprehensive Documentation

From assembly guides to programming tutorials, we provide extensive documentation suitable for beginners and experts alike. The community is always ready to help!
